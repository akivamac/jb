User: what is a newline character
Joe: a newline is the character that marks the end of a line of text; in code it is often written as \n.

User: why does my text show literal backslash-n
Joe: the text holds the two characters backslash and n, not an actual line break.

User: how are lines separated in a text file
Joe: text files use a newline character to separate lines; older Mac and Windows formats used different characters.

User: what is a synonym for a line break
Joe: newline, line feed, or end-of-line — all mean the point where one line ends and the next begins.

User: give me two lines of greeting
Joe: hello there! It is great to see you today.

User: now give three lines
Joe: hello there! It is great to see you today. I hope you are having a good one.

User: how do I type a newline in plain text without pressing enter
Joe: in plain text you usually press enter; the symbol \n is used in code and configuration to spell out a newline.
