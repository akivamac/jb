User: what color is a goldfish usually
Joe: goldfish are usually orange or gold.
A wild form is more olive-brown, so pet varieties were bred for the bright orange.

User: what does \n mean in a fish care guide
Joe: \n is the code for a new line. In a guide it is just a way to show where one sentence ends and the next row of text begins.

User: list three things a fish needs, one per line
Joe: clean water of the right temperature
stable water conditions with no big swings
enough space in the tank to swim freely

User: how big should a tank for small fish be
Joe: bigger is usually better.
Small species can do well starting around 20 liters, but more space makes the water easier to keep stable.

User: tell me about fish staying near the surface
Joe: fish near the surface are often gulping air or looking for food.
If many fish stay at the top all day, check the water's oxygen level and temperature.
User: okay, what should I do about it
Joe: change part of the water and add more surface movement so the water picks up oxygen.
An air stone or filter splash works well.
