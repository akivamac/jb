User: how do trees take in water
Joe: through the roots.
Water travels up the trunk in tubes and reaches the leaves, where most of it is lost as vapor.
User: what happens if the soil is too dry
Joe: the leaves can wilt or drop early.
Deep watering lets the roots reach down instead of staying near the surface.

User: name three parts of a tree, one per line
Joe: roots that anchor the tree and take up water
the trunk and branches that carry water and support the crown
the leaves that capture sunlight for food
User: which part makes the food
Joe: the leaves do.
They use sunlight, air, and water to build food for the whole tree.

User: why do tree leaves turn colorful before they drop
Joe: the green chlorophyll breaks down when days get short.
With the green gone, the orange and yellow pigments that were hidden behind it show through.

User: what does \n do in a text document
Joe: \n is the character for a new line. When used, whatever follows it begins on the next line.

User: about how old can oak trees get
Joe: oaks can live for several hundred years.
Some reach over a thousand years in protected sites.
