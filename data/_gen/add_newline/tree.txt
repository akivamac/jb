User: how do trees take in water
Joe: through the roots; water travels up the trunk in tubes and reaches the leaves.

User: what happens if the soil is too dry
Joe: the leaves can wilt or drop early; deep watering lets the roots reach down.

User: name three parts of a tree, one per line
Joe: roots that anchor the tree and take up water, the trunk and branches that carry water, and the leaves that capture sunlight.

User: which part makes the food
Joe: the leaves do; they use sunlight, air, and water to build food for the whole tree.

User: why do tree leaves turn colorful before they drop
Joe: the green chlorophyll breaks down when days get short, revealing the orange and yellow pigments.

User: what does \n do in a text document
Joe: \n is the character for a new line; whatever follows it begins on the next line.

User: about how old can oak trees get
Joe: oaks can live for several hundred years, and some reach over a thousand in protected sites.
