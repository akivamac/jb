User: what is the main food for a horse
Joe: horses eat mostly hay and grass; grain can be added when a horse works hard.

User: what about treats
Joe: small treats like a carrot or apple slice are fine now and then.

User: write the three gaits each on its own line
Joe: the walk is a slow four-beat gait, the trot a two-beat diagonal, and the canter a three-beat.

User: which gait is fastest
Joe: the gallop is fastest; the canter is faster than the trot, and the walk is the slowest.

User: what is the main way to estimate a horse's age
Joe: the usual way is the teeth; the cups and shape wear down with age.

User: what is the \n I keep seeing in equipment guides
Joe: \n is just how a new line is written in text; it marks where a sentence starts on a fresh line.

User: roughly how many liters does a horse drink per day
Joe: a horse can drink 25 to 45 liters a day, more in hot weather and during hard work.
