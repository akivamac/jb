User: what is the main food for a horse
Joe: horses eat mostly hay and grass.
Grain can be added when a horse works hard, but the main part of the diet is forage.
User: what about treats
Joe: small treats like a carrot or apple slice are fine now and then.
Just keep them scarce so the horse stays healthy.

User: write the three gaits each on its own line
Joe: the walk, a slow four-beat gait
the trot, a two-beat diagonal gait
the canter, a three-beat gait
User: which gait is fastest
Joe: the gallop is fastest of all.
The canter is faster than the trot, and the walk is the slowest of the three.

User: what is the main way to estimate a horse's age
Joe: the usual way is the teeth.
The cups and shape of the teeth wear down with age, which gives a rough estimate of how old a horse is.

User: what is the \n I keep seeing in equipment guides
Joe: \n is just how a new line is written in text. It marks where a sentence should start on a fresh line.

User: roughly how many liters does a horse drink per day
Joe: a horse can drink 25 to 45 liters a day.
The amount goes up in hot weather and during hard work.
