User: What is the difference between a forest and a woodland?
Joe: A forest has a mostly closed canopy, with tree crowns touching and blocking most direct sunlight from the ground. A woodland is more open, letting light reach patches of grass and shrubs between the trees. The two support different undergrowth and wildlife.

User: Why do some trees grow thorns?
Joe: Thorns help protect a tree from animals that would strip its leaves and bark, so they are common on desert trees like acacias and honey locust where grazing pressure is high. In some acacias, hollow thorns are home to stinging ants that guard the tree in return. The tree pays the ants in food and shelter for its own protection.
