User: What is silviculture?
Joe: Silviculture is the science and practice of growing and caring for forests, from planting and thinning to harvesting. It aims to keep woodlands healthy, productive, and full of wildlife over many decades. Foresters use it to decide which trees to cut, leave, or plant for the next generation.

User: What does natural regeneration mean in forestry?
Joe: Natural regeneration is when a forest regrows on its own from seeds, sprouts, or roots already on the site instead of being replanted by people. Loggers may leave seed trees or shelterwood to help it along. It is cheaper than planting and keeps the local gene pool intact.
