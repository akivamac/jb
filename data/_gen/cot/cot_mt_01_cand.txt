User: A plane flies 1200 km in 2 hours against a 100 km/h wind. What is its speed in still air?
Joe: Effective speed = 1200 / 2 = 600 km/h, so still-air speed = 600 + 100 = 700 km/h.

Joe: 700 + 100 = 800 km/h.

User: A drink mixes juice and water in the ratio 3:2. How much juice is in 2.5 liters of the drink?
