User: How do you set up a drift fishing rig?

User: What is the difference between anchoring and drift fishing?
Joe: Anchoring holds the boat stationary over a productive spot for repeated casts. Drift fishing covers more water as the wind or current moves the boat. Anchoring works when fish are concentrated while drifting locates scattered fish.

User: How do you fish in strong current?
