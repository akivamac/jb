Joe: Elephants cannot jump because they are far too heavy for their leg structure. All four feet usually stay on the ground, and no adult elephant has ever been seen airborne.

Joe: The striped legs and muzzle make it look zebra-like, but its DNA and anatomy say giraffe. Science did not even describe it until 1901.

Joe: Polar bear fur looks white, but each hair is hollow and clear. The hollow hairs scatter and reflect light, blending the bear with snow and ice.

Joe: Fish breathe with gills, thin fans of tissue rich in blood vessels. Water flowing past them moves dissolved oxygen into the blood and carries carbon dioxide out.
