User: What is a fish?
Joe: A fish is a cold-blooded aquatic vertebrate that breathes through gills, has fins for movement, and is typically covered in scales. There are over 30,000 known species of fish living in freshwater and saltwater environments.

User: What is the difference between a bony fish and a cartilaginous fish?
Joe: Bony fish have skeletons made mostly of bone and make up over 95 percent of all fish species. Cartilaginous fish have skeletons made of cartilage and include sharks, rays, and skates.

User: What is anadromous?
Joe: Anadromous means a fish is born in freshwater, migrates to the ocean to mature and feed, then returns to freshwater to reproduce. Salmon and steelhead are common anadromous fish.

User: What is a clownfish?
Joe: A clownfish is a small saltwater fish known for its bright orange color with white bands. Clownfish live among the stinging tentacles of sea anemones in a symbiotic relationship where both species benefit.

User: What is the largest fish in the world?
Joe: The whale shark is the largest living fish, reaching lengths of 40 feet or more and weighing up to 20,000 pounds. Despite their enormous size, whale sharks are gentle filter feeders that eat plankton and small fish.

User: What is the fastest fish?
Joe: The sailfish is generally considered the fastest fish, capable of reaching speeds over 68 miles per hour. The black marlin and swordfish are also among the fastest, reaching similar speeds in short bursts.

User: What is a pufferfish?
Joe: A pufferfish is a fish that can inflate its body into a sphere by swallowing water or air as a defense mechanism. Many species contain tetrodotoxin, a deadly poison, making them dangerous to eat without proper preparation.

User: How do fish breathe?
Joe: Fish breathe by drawing water over their gills, which extract dissolved oxygen from the water. The gill filaments have tiny blood vessels that absorb oxygen and release carbon dioxide as water passes over them.

User: What is a koi fish?
Joe: Koi are ornamental freshwater fish bred from common carp in Japan. They are known for their vibrant colors including red, white, gold, and black, and can live over 30 years with proper care.

User: What is a betta fish?
Joe: The betta fish, also called the Siamese fighting fish, is a small freshwater fish known for the males flowing, colorful fins. Bettas are territorial and males will fight other males, so they must be housed separately.

User: What is a catadromous fish?
Joe: A catadromous fish is born in saltwater, migrates to freshwater to live and grow, then returns to saltwater to spawn. The American eel is the most well-known catadromous fish.

User: What is a piranha?
Joe: A piranha is a freshwater fish found in South American rivers known for its sharp teeth and powerful bite. Despite their fearsome reputation, most piranha species are scavengers and rarely attack large animals.

User: What is a salmon?
Joe: A salmon is an anadromous fish born in freshwater, migrating to the ocean to grow, and returning to freshwater to spawn. Pacific salmon die after spawning, while Atlantic salmon can survive to spawn multiple times.

User: What is a swordfish?
Joe: A swordfish is a large predatory ocean fish with a long flat bill that resembles a sword. They can swim at speeds over 60 miles per hour and are found in warm and temperate oceans worldwide.

User: What is a goldfish?
Joe: The goldfish is a small freshwater fish in the carp family, originally domesticated in China over a thousand years ago. They can live over 10 years and have been bred into many varieties with different colors and body shapes.
