User: Why is my fish tank cloudy?
Joe: Cloudy water in a fish tank is usually caused by a bacterial bloom during cycling, overfeeding, inadequate filtration, or disturbance of the substrate. White cloudy water is typically bacteria. Green cloudy water is an algae bloom from excess light or nutrients. Regular water changes and proper maintenance clear it.

User: How do fish breathe underwater?
Joe: Fish breathe by drawing water over their gills. Water enters the mouth and passes over four pairs of gill filaments covered in tiny blood vessels called lamellae. Oxygen diffuses from the water into the blood, and carbon dioxide diffuses out. Most fish must keep water flowing over their gills.

User: What is a fish labyrinth organ?
Joe: The labyrinth organ is a specialized breathing structure found in anabantids like bettas and gouramis. It allows these fish to breathe atmospheric air directly. This adaptation lets them survive in low-oxygen water like stagnant ponds and rice paddies where other fish cannot live.

User: Why are my fish hiding?
Joe: Fish hide when stressed, scared, sick, or adjusting to a new environment. Common causes include new tank mates, bright lighting, lack of hiding spots, poor water quality, or bullying. Provide caves, plants, and decorations. If hiding persists, test water parameters.

User: Why is my fish swimming upside down?
Joe: Upside-down swimming usually indicates swim bladder disease, which can be caused by overfeeding, constipation, bacterial infection, or physical injury. Fasting for 2 to 3 days and then feeding a deshelled pea can help. If it persists, antibiotics may be needed.

User: What is a fish bloom feed?
Joe: Bloom feeding is a technique where live cultures of microorganisms are grown in the tank to provide constant natural food for fry and small fish. Algae blooms, infusoria, and vinegar eels create a living food source that fish can graze on throughout the day.

User: Why do male bettas fight?
Joe: Male bettas are territorial and aggressive toward other male bettas because they compete for territory and mates in their natural shallow-water habitat. They flare their gills and fins to appear larger and may fight to the death if confined together. Each male needs its own space.

User: What temperature do tropical fish need?
Joe: Most tropical freshwater fish thrive between 74 and 80 degrees Fahrenheit. Specific species vary: discus prefer 82 to 88, while danios tolerate 65 to 75. A reliable heater and thermometer are essential. Temperature fluctuations stress fish and make them susceptible to disease.

User: What is the best water conditioner for aquariums?
Joe: Water conditioners like Seachem Prime or API Tap Water Conditioner remove chlorine, chloramine, and heavy metals from tap water. They also detoxify ammonia and nitrite temporarily. Use whenever adding tap water during water changes or when filling a new tank.

User: How often should I do water changes?
Joe: Change 10 to 25 percent of aquarium water weekly for most tanks. heavily stocked tanks may need more frequent changes. Always use a gravel vacuum to remove debris from the substrate. Replace with dechlorinated water at the same temperature as the tank.

User: What is live rock in a saltwater aquarium?
Joe: Live rock is rock from the ocean or artificially seeded rock colonized by beneficial bacteria, algae, corals, and microorganisms. It serves as the biological filter in reef aquariums, providing surface area for nitrifying bacteria and habitat for small creatures like copepods and worms.
